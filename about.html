About

About me

I'm currently a Master's student at Northeastern University pursuing an M.S in Quantitative Finance, and am interested in full time roles starting after my expected graduation date in December of 2026. I'm fascinated with markets, their structure and behavior, and the analysis thereof by quantitative and qualitative means.

Background

My interest in markets, which I view as a foundational pillar of social science as they constitute the organization of human production and consumption, originated early on in my education and motivated my choice of undergraduate studies. I majored in Economics at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, and picked up a math minor along the way to build up my quantitative skillset. While at UMass I participated in the Economics Undergraduate Research Assistant (EURA) program and got exposure to research, but I found that the methods I learned about in Econometrics and a modelling course in the math department intrigued me more. After earning my Bachelor's degree I elected to continue my studies for the time being, and began to pursue my Master's. It's been intellectually rewarding thus far thanks to a few great professors I've had at Northeastern, wherein my studies have very much felt like a natural extension of my undergraduate subjects. Moreover, I've learned about the industry and job opportunities in Quantitative Finance, and that has ignited an interest in this incredibly dynamic field.

What I focus on

I'd say my interests are broad and varied, but I'm quite interested in options markets and the mathematical motivations of different pricing models. I like to form stories to gain a deeper understanding of the function of markets, and believe that the deepest insights originate from quantitative and qualitative foundations.
