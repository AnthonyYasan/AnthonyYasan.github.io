I’d describe myself as someone who likes to learn how complex systems work, and that has materialized itself as a years long academic and personal interest in Economics and Mathematics. After I earned my Bachelor’s degree I learned about the field of Quantitative Finance and thought it was the most natural extension of those interests in a future career. Quant seems to me a dynamic intellectual environment to build a career in, and this website is intended as a showcase of work samples for those interested. Aside from intellectual pursuits, I enjoy outdoor activities such as hiking and skiing, going out with friends and family, and participating in strength sports.
